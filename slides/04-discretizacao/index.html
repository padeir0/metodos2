Discretização da EDP

Malha $x_i=ih$, $y_j=jh$, $t_n=n\,\Delta t$, com $u^n_{i,j}\approx u(x_i,y_j,t_n)$. Diferenças centrais:

\[ u_{tt}\approx\frac{u^{n+1}_{i,j}-2u^n_{i,j}+u^{n-1}_{i,j}}{\Delta t^2},\qquad u_{xx}+u_{yy}\approx\frac{L^n_{i,j}}{h^2} \] \[ L^n_{i,j}=u^n_{i+1,j}+u^n_{i-1,j}+u^n_{i,j+1}+u^n_{i,j-1}-4u^n_{i,j} \]

Substituindo na equação da onda:

\[ u_{tt} = c^2 (u_{xx} + u_{yy}) \Rightarrow \frac{u^{n+1}_{i,j}-2u^n_{i,j}+u^{n-1}_{i,j}}{\Delta t^2} = \frac{c^2}{h^2} L^n_{i,j} \]

Isolando $u^{n+1}$, com $r=c\,\Delta t/h$:

\[ \boxed{\,u^{n+1}_{i,j}=2u^n_{i,j}-u^{n-1}_{i,j}+r^2L^n_{i,j}\,} \]
